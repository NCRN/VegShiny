Data by Park and Species

These figures display estimates of the abundance, size, and proportion of monitoring plots occupied by a species in each park. They show the mean and the 95% confidence intervals for the selected species. A graph and data table are available for download.

Display Summary Statistics: Toggles whether each species' summary statistics are shown on the figure.

Comparison Data: Adds a second dataset to compare against:

  • Park (compare two parks)
  • Growth Stage (compare trees vs. saplings vs. tree seedlings of the same species)
  • Time (compare two cycles)

Error Bar Thickness: Slider adjusting the thickness of the confidence-interval error bars.
